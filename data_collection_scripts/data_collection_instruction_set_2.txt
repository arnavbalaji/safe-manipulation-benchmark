Notes:
- A window displays the health of the objects you manipulate. This health 
reflects the amount of damage incurred. Damage is bad, so aim to minimize 
damage whenever possible.

- Please avoid unnecessary or random robot motions. 
Execute the task using deliberate, efficient movements, as you would 
naturally do yourself.



Spacemouse tips:
- Use small and smooth motions of spacemouse, avoid big and sudden movements.

- Rotating the robot gripper can get a bit tricky, so avoid that if possible.

- At times, the SpaceMouse may cause sudden or abrupt robot motions. 
If this happens, slow down, try small adjustments or alternative motions, 
and continue once the movement feels smooth again.
